Project: PRJNA437010
"Information on the gut microbiome of piglets during the weaning transition is still lacking. Here, we performed 16S rRNA gene sequencing to uncover the microbial community structure and membership of piglet gut microbiota during the weaning transition. The 16S rRNA gene sequencing revealed significant composition of the fecal microbiota between nursing and weaned piglets."
